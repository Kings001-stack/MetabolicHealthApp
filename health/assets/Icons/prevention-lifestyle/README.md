# Prevention & Lifestyle Icons

Use for prevention, habits, exercises, sleep, stress/mood, and coaching content.

Suggested files:
- risk-assessment.json (#009688 or #2196F3)
- questionnaire.json (#009688 or #2196F3)
- scorecard.json (#4CAF50)
- light-bulb.json (#FFC107)
- idea.json (#FFC107)
- health-tips.json (#FFC107)
- yoga-pose.json (#4CAF50)
- workout-home.json (#4CAF50 or #FF9800)
- stretching.json (#4CAF50)
- sleep.json (#607D8B)
- stress-mood.json (#673AB7 or #3F51B5)
- meditation-audio.json (#673AB7 or #3F51B5)

Guidelines:
- favor calm, subtle animations for wellness topics (sleep, meditation).
- Use bolder motion for exercise/workout to indicate activity.

Primary accents:
- Sleep: Blue Gray `#607D8B`
- Meditation / Calm: Deep Purple `#673AB7` or Indigo `#3F51B5`
- Exercise / Workout: Green `#4CAF50` or Orange `#FF9800` depending on context
- Tips / Light-bulb / ideas: Amber `#FFC107`
- Questionnaires / Risk: Teal `#009688` or Info Blue `#2196F3`

Dark mode adjustments:
- Sleep: `#78909C`
- Meditation: `#9575CD` (Deep Purple 300) or `#5C6BC0` (Indigo 400)
- Exercise: `#66BB6A` (Green 400) or `#FFB74D` (Orange 300)
- Tips/Ideas: `#FFD54F`
- Neutrals: `#E0E0E0`

Notes:
- Keep backgrounds transparent; cards provide backgrounds.
- Use consistent color per topic to help users recognize categories quickly.
