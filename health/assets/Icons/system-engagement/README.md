# System & Engagement Icons

Use for alerts, achievements, gamification, calls-to-action, and general engagement UI.

Suggested files:
- emergency-alert.json (#E53935)
- warning-sign.json (#FFB300)
- exclamation.json (optional) (#FFB300)
- challenge-badge.json (#4CAF50)
- trophy.json (#FFC107)
- daily-goal.json (#4CAF50)
- medal.json (#FFC107)
- badge-earned.json (#4CAF50)
- streak.json (#4CAF50 or #FFC107)
- quiz.json (#2196F3)
- fun-qa.json (#2196F3)
- question-mark.json (#2196F3)
- premium-star.json (#FFC107)
- crown.json (#FFC107)
- ad-banner.json (#2196F3 or neutral #757575)
- phone.json (#2196F3)
- emergency-call.json (#E53935)
- doctor.json (#2196F3 or #00897B)

Guidelines:
- Alerts should have accessible contrast and restrained animation.
- Badges and stars can be playful but keep sizes modest in lists.

Statuses and calls-to-action:
- Success: `#4CAF50`
- Warning: `#FFB300`
- Error/Critical: `#E53935`
- Info: `#2196F3`
- Premium/Star: `#FFC107`
- Emergency/Call: `#E53935`
- Providers/Doctor: `#2196F3` or Teal `#00897B`

Dark mode adjustments:
- Neutral icons (secondary): `#E0E0E0`
- Success: `#66BB6A`
- Warning: `#FFD54F`
- Error: `#EF5350`
- Info: `#42A5F5`

Notes:
- Keep backgrounds transparent; let screens/cards style the background.
- Prefer single-color or limited-color icons for badges and lists.
